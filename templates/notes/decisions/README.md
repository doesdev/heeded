# Decisions

One decision per file, named `NNNN-kebab-title.md`, numbered
in order. A record is never edited to mean something else; a
later record amends or supersedes it and says so in `Status`.

```
# NNNN: Title

Date: (from `date -u +%Y-%m-%dT%H:%MZ`)
Status: accepted | rejected | amended by NNNN | superseded by NNNN

## Context

What forced a choice.

## Decision

What was chosen, and why not the alternatives.

## Consequences

What this costs and what it rules out.
```
